Our history

The service record

2009

Founded on Mitchell Hammock

Oviedo Car Care is a family-owned and operated business, founded in 2009 by Craig and Sandra Leiss along with Joe and Zelia Miranda.

2012

Anthony joins the family

After 21 years working in corporate automotive repair, Anthony Pujols joined the Oviedo Car Care family, first as a technician and then as a service adviser. Anthony loved working for a family-owned business, where he could actually make a difference in the day-to-day and in the relationships built here.

2018

Anthony & Amy take the keys

In 2018, Anthony and his wife, Amy, became the new owners of Oviedo Car Care.

→

Still here, still busy

We pride ourselves on fair prices, timely repairs, and long-lasting, small-town relationships. We believe honesty and integrity in our work are the whole reason we're still here, and still busy. We love saving our customers money, and we're betting that's what brings you back the next time something comes up.

The owners

Anthony & Amy Pujols

Anthony — The Standard in the Bays

Owner, Anthony Pujols, has been fixing cars his entire career. He is Lexus certified and ASE certified. He brings his excellent diagnostic skills along with a tenacious desire to solve any problem presented to him.

Amy — The Standard at the Counter

Owner, Amy Pujols, has been a customer of automotive repair shops up until meeting Anthony. She brings with her a deep desire to assure that her customers never feel the way she felt when visiting a repair shop. Honesty, transparency, clear explanations, and never talking down to or judging customers is a priority for her.
